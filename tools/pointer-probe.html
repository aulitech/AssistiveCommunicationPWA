The trip — does leaving get reported?

  1. Rest on the box. The bar starts filling.
  2. Before it finishes, move to the on-screen keyboard.
  3. Type 1 — that stamps the log and proves keystrokes still arrive.
  4. Come back and read the log.

Repeat with 2 for the menu bar and 3 for another app's window: being covered by a floating panel and leaving the window outright need not behave alike.
