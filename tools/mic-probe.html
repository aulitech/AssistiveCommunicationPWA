Peri microphone probe

Which microphone the speech recogniser is actually listening to.

The trip

You need two microphones you can tell apart. A headset and the built-in one will do.

  1. Press Allow microphones. The list below fills in with a live meter each.
  2. Speak. Watch which meters move.
  3. Make one device hot and the others cold. Speak quietly right into a headset boom held away from the laptop, or cover the built-in mic.
  4. Press Start recogniser and do step 3 again.

The recogniser transcribes what the system default hears, or what the hot device hears. The meters say which was which.
