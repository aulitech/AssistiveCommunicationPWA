# The message card

> One of the decisions behind Peri, moved out of [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), which keeps the map of
> the source and the rules that hold everywhere. What is written here is why the code is the shape it is.

**The message box and the question box are cards**, shaped like a chat app's input box: the words, and inside the card along the bottom a row of everything that works on them. They replaced a layout in which every control rode one of the box's borders — the modes centred on the upper one, the actions at its right, the slot that emptied the box at the left of the lower one, the edit strip centred on it — which saved the grid a row of height and cost a good deal of positioning arithmetic and a box whose edges were crowded with things painted over them.

- **On top, one row, with the modes centred.** The clipboard at the far left, the modes — microphone, edit, Rest, auto-speak, and please after them — in a middle column between two equal ones, so they sit on the card's centre whatever is either side, and the language, the voice and, in edit mode, Delete at the right: as far from Save as the card goes. **Delete is unseen until there is something to delete** — a phrase off the board rather than a new one — and hidden rather than taken away, so its place is kept and the language and the voice never slide under a resting pointer as it comes and goes.
- **Under it, the line the words are written on.** The slot that empties the box — Clear, Undo, or in edit mode *start a new phrase* — at its left end, centred on the words however many lines they run to, then the box, with the primary — Speak, or Save — **right after the last word**, filled in the accent as a chat app fills Send and drawn only once there are words. `ui/after-text.tsx` finds where the words end: a never-seen copy of the box holds the same words and then a space the control's width, and the control goes where that space lands — a rem and a few pixels after the last word, so it reads as standing after the words rather than as one more of them, or at the start of the next line, level with the words, when the last has no room, and the box grows to hold that line. The question card's line is the same, with Ask (suggest answers) after the question's last word, filled in the accent as Speak is — it is to the question what Speak is to the message — and the live microphone at the line's right end.
- **A control that moves with every letter is a new hazard for a gaze.** It could land under a pointer resting on the words, and its going — Save, the moment the phrase is saved — leaves the words under a pointer that was resting on it, where a rest places the caret and counts as choosing something (it wiped the mark on the new phrase and the toast saying it was saved). So each time it lands or goes, if the pointer is where it is or was, nothing may be chosen until the pointer moves (`holdIfUnderPointer`); and placed again where it already stood, it has not arrived anywhere. Typing was covered already — the grid narrowing to a typed word holds every dwell — so the browser test drives the case that is not: the screen rewrapping the words under a resting, drifting pointer.
- **A narrow card stacks the row**: the modes on a line of their own, centred, with the clipboard and the values under them at either end. Asked of the card's own width (`@container message`) rather than the screen's, since the card narrows when the question opens beside it. The values wrap inside themselves if they have to, and the Rest bar is shorter on a phone, so everything fits a 360px screen.
- **The gaps are target-separation numbers.** Every control carries an invisible area 6px past its edges, so two closer than 12px share a rest: 1.25rem between controls, 0.75rem between the modes and between lines. `tests/e2e/layout.spec.ts` measures it at 360, 390 and 1280px, in both modes — every control inside the card, none within 12px of another, the modes on the card's centre.
- **What is being edited is at the right end of the line the words are written on**, beside the words it is about, 0.75rem clear of them so Save and the category trigger never share a rest. A narrow card has no room for both, so there it takes a line of its own under the words, still at the right. It was a row of the card's own under the line.
- **Opening or closing the question holds the dwells.** The question's card opens above the message's on a narrow screen and beside it on a wide one, and either way the message card — and the microphone in its row — moves out from under the pointer that rested on it. See [Settling after the screen moves](settling-after-the-screen-moves.md).
- **The language and the voice have no ground of their own**: the card's shows through them, and the edge alone says each is a control.
- **The menu is outside the card, in the upper right corner** — about the app rather than the message, and where an app's menu is looked for. Back stands in the same corner of every panel the menu opens and holds the dwells as it closes one, so the pointer it leaves over the menu does not open it again; the busy indicator stands left of the menu rather than over it. The keyboard, where it is offered, is at the bar's left end.
- **Clear is a heavy X in a muted red** (`--clear-red`, the danger red let down towards the grey): it throws words away, but less finally than the bin, which is the full red.
- **Focus is always in a box**: the question's while it is open, the message's otherwise — `focusBox` in `topbar.tsx`. Put there as the question opens and as it closes, and put back whenever focus falls to nothing (a panel or dialog closing, a press on empty space), so a key typed always lands in one of the two; the message box gets back the caret its own state says it had, Chrome having moved an unfocused box's caret to the start without saying so. Focus moved elsewhere by Tab, or into another field, is left there. No control takes focus on a press either — see `keepFocus` in `ui/dwell.ts`.
- **The menu is a bare glyph**, with no ground or edge of its own, like the controls in the cards.
- **The card is the box.** The textarea inside it has no ground, edge or focus of its own; the card has a ground a step up from the bar's (`--card-ground`, the darkest that keeps the muted grey at 7:1), a soft edge (`--card-edge`), rounded corners and a shadow, and says focus on its edge with `:focus-within` (`--card-focus`). Edit mode dashes the message card's edge in the accent; the question card's edge is dashed always — somebody else's words — and solid accent while the microphone is on.
- **Every control in either card is the microphone's size**, 2rem with a 1.2rem glyph, so the row reads as one set. `tests/app/message.test.tsx` holds it.
