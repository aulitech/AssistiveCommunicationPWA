# Presentation mode

A window of its own that shows what Peri says, plays the audio, and — a dwell away — the camera, so that one window can be shared in a video call or turned towards a room. The toggle is the last in the message card's row of modes, after please (`present-toggle`) — **not on a phone** (`max-width: 700px`), where a second window is a tab nobody can share beside the board and the row has no room for it at 360px; the window is `talk/presentation.tsx`, opened by `talk/use-presentation.ts`; and what is said reaches it through the **stage** in `voice/speech.ts`.

- **A window rather than something drawn inside Peri**, because the point is to share it on its own: what is said, the face and the sound, and none of the board. It is the app at `/presentation`, chosen in `App.tsx` by its address, so it is read through the same board as the Peri that opened it — its picture and its dwell times are this person's.
- **Opening can be refused, and it is said when it is.** A browser opens a window only off the back of a press, and a dwell is none — the same refusal a link meets, answered the same way: a toast saying to allow pop-ups for Peri. On a gaze rig that sends real clicks it simply opens.
- **The stage is installed from above.** `speech.ts` sits in `voice/`, below the screens, so it cannot import the window; the window puts a `Stage` on itself (`periStage`), tells Peri it is ready with a message, and Peri hands `setStage` a stage that reaches through the window afresh each time it is asked. Nothing is spoken through a window that has not said it is ready, or that Peri did not open.
- **What is shown is what was said** — translated, with please, its markup off — handed to the stage in `say()`, once the words are settled. A voice sample in the picker is the one thing said that is not shown (`SpeakOptions.sample`): it is said to the person choosing, not to anybody watching.
- **The audio plays in the window**, so sharing that window shares the sound. **Only an account's audio can**: a browser gives a page no way to route its own synthesiser, so a device voice is always said from Peri itself. The guide says so.
- **It must never cost a phrase.** A browser plays sound only in a window somebody has pressed in, and nobody has pressed in this one; a window can close or reload under Peri at any moment. Any of these — `play` rejecting, or the window being gone — and the clip is played in Peri's own window instead. The window says when that has happened, until it is pressed in once.
- **The words are made to be read from across a room, and sit in the lower third of the window, as captions do** — over the camera's foot rather than its face, and in the same place whether the camera is on or not. The newest is large at the foot of the band, scaled with the window since that is what somebody sizes to their screen; what came before is smaller and muted above it, and **each new thing said scrolls the rest up**, fading out at the top of the band rather than cut off — smoothly, unless somebody has asked their system for less motion. Twenty are kept, which is more than the band ever shows. The band is scrolled by the page and never by hand, so it is not a surface a dwell has to reach. Over a picture or the camera it is a band of the page's own ground.
- **The camera is as large as the window allows, its shape kept** — `object-fit: contain`, so one side meets the window and the other fits within it, with the page's ground showing round it. **The background picture goes while the camera is on**: two pictures would compete, and the face is the one being watched. Never stretched, never cropped. The picture stays in that window: nothing records it, keeps it or sends it — the privacy policy has a section saying so.
- **The picture behind it** — Settings → Presentation background — is kept by `core/store/presentation.ts` under a key of its own, not in the settings: settings ride inside the board, into every backup and every push, and a picture is hundreds of times the size of everything else in them. It is drawn again at no more than 1920 pixels on its longest side and kept as a JPEG before anything tries to keep it — at a lower quality, and then smaller, until it is no larger than a clip may be (`menu/picture.ts`) — and the window follows a new one live through the storage event.
- **The picture goes to the user's other devices, and into no backup.** It was this device's alone at first; it is a thing somebody sets up once and expects to find on the tablet as well as the laptop. **Not inside the board**, which is written whole on every change: the board carries only which picture (`Snapshot.picture`, a name worked out from its bytes) and when it was chosen (`pictureAt`), and **the newer of two devices' stands, whichever board is newer** — the rule the keys follow, for their reason (`pictureFrom` in `core/sync.ts`). The picture itself goes up once, **as a clip among the audio** (`sync/picture.ts`, driven by `talk/use-picture.ts`): locked the same way, at an address only the passphrase leads to, and named in the clip index, so *Stop and erase the copy* takes it too. Its address carries when it was chosen as well as which, so a picture taken off the server as replaced can never be one chosen again since.
- **What is owed is written down** (`PictureRecord`, never sent): up, for a picture chosen here; down, for one the board names that is not here — **checked against its name before it is shown**; and off, for one replaced since it went up, so the server is not left holding every picture somebody tried. Each is tried whenever which picture changes and on a slow beat after, so a device closed half way finishes the job when it opens. A board naming a new picture leaves the old one up until the new one has arrived; one naming none takes it down at once.
- **A picture chosen before pictures travelled** is named as chosen at the beginning of time, so any choice made since, anywhere, stands over it.
- **The window goes when Peri does**, on `pagehide`: a window left behind would show what was said to a call nobody is speaking in any more. Closed from its own side, it says nothing as it goes, so Peri asks once a second and puts the toggle out.
