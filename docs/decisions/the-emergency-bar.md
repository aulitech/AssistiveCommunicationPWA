# The emergency bar

> One of the decisions behind Peri, moved out of [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), which keeps the map of
> the source and the rules that hold everywhere. What is written here is why the code is the shape it is.

The blue bar is the one surface somebody reaches for without reading it, so which button sits where is theirs to set. **It is not red**, and `--urgent` is not `--danger`: red in this app means destruction — delete a phrase, empty the board, sign out — and a red bar along the bottom said *careful* where it needed to say *here, now*. **In edit mode, the emergency phrase open in the box carries a bin against its button's left edge and a drag pad against its right**, as a phrase open in the grid does — `ItemTool` in `talk/item-tool.tsx`, one for all of them — and the pad behaves exactly like the category tabs': it lifts the phrase blue, a dwell on another button drops it there, the pad again puts it back, and a mouse can drag the pad instead. Both come out of `ui/reorder`. The bin asks first (`AskFirst` in `talk/editors.tsx`).

- **The order is by phrase id, and empty means the order Peri ships.** Ids, so rewording a phrase leaves it exactly where it was put — the whole point of arranging a bar is reaching it without looking. Unlike the categories there is no second arrangement to switch back to: the shipped order is the order they happen to be written in, and nobody is looking for it back.
- **A phrase added later lands at the end.** `orderByIds` ranks the ids it knows and leaves the rest as they came, so adding an emergency phrase does not rewrite an arrangement, and an id naming a deleted phrase is skipped rather than leaving a hole.
- **There is no reorder mode, and so nothing to leave armed.** There was one, a toggle at the end of the bar beside add, kept separate from the tabs' so tidying them could not arm the bar somebody speaks with. A pad on the one phrase open in the box does the same by construction: a tab in the air and a phrase in the air are two `useReorder`s, and what is in the air is put down when another phrase is opened, its words change, or edit mode ends.
- **Add stays right after the last button**, as a new tab follows the last tab, and is the bar's only tool. A new phrase is another phrase in the box, so starting one puts down whatever was in the air.

## Words the bar cuts off

**On a narrow screen a button can be too small for its words** — a phone held upright fits six a word or two wide. So while any button's words are cut off, resting on one shows its words **whole and large in the middle of the phrase table** (`onPeek` in `EmergencyBar`, drawn by `PhraseGrid` as `.emergency-peek`), and they go when the pointer leaves. Measured on every change to the bar and every resize of it — wider or taller than the button shows — so a screen where nothing is cut off shows nothing. In the bar's own colours, on a card over the phrases, and letting the pointer through: it is not a target, and the button is still the thing being rested on.
