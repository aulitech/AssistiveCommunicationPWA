# Undo and Redo

> One of the decisions behind Peri, moved out of [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), which keeps the map of
> the source and the rules that hold everywhere. What is written here is why the code is the shape it is.

**What is done to the board can be taken back and done again**: a phrase saved (made, reworded, kept off a record, or added to the category showing), a phrase deleted or taken out of a category, a phrase on the emergency bar deleted, and a category deleted. Undo and Redo are after Paste at the far left of the message card's top row, in both modes, since a category can be deleted from its tab in either. Each names what it will reach (`Undo: Deleted “Tea please”`), and is quiet rather than away with nothing to reach, so nothing moves along the row as they come and go. **Not on a phone** (`PHONE` in `ui/media.ts`), and neither is the clipboard beside them: the top line of a phone has room for the modes and the menu and nothing more, and asked for that way, a delete on a phone is final. On a narrow card that is not a phone — large text on a small tablet, the card's breakpoint being in rem — they are at the start of the line under.

- **It replaced a single undo.** There was one step back from a delete, offered in the slot that starts a new phrase and gone the moment anything else was written or edit mode was left. A save could not be taken back at all, nor a category deleted with everything filed in it.
- **A change is kept as the difference it made**, both ways — `diffStore` and `applyDelta` in `core/history.ts` — rather than as the board before and after. A board is tens of kilobytes; fifty of them twice over is more than a device's storage would hold for one record, beside the board itself.
- **The difference is applied to the board as it stands now**, so what came since is left alone: a message kept in Library, another category's phrases, a board arriving from another device. Putting the whole board back as it was would have taken all of that with it. A phrase comes back to its place among the ones there now, and an id the board no longer holds is simply not there to move.
- **What the screen holds outside the board goes with it**: the count a deleted phrase took with it (`use` on the change), put back by Undo and taken again by Redo, and a hidden tab whose category was deleted (`hiddenTab`), hidden again when it comes back.
- **Undo leaves the change on screen**: the tab it was made on, where that is still there; the phrase it was about open in the box in edit mode — where an undone delete was when the bin took it — and marked on the board; and a toast saying what was undone. A phrase open in the box that the change took off the board leaves the box.
- **How a change is caught** (`talk/use-history.ts`): saving and deleting write the board through state, which lands in the next render. So each save and delete says what it is (`record`), and the difference is taken in the render that shows the new board, against the board as it was drawn when the change was said. **A change that came to nothing is let go at the first render after it**, rather than pinned on whatever changes the board next — `tests/talk/use-history.test.tsx` holds that.
- **Kept on this device, under the account, across a reload** (`peri_history`, through `storageKey` and `writeKey`) — chosen on request over a history that lasted only until Peri was closed. **The last fifty changes**; a new change forgets what was undone. A factory reset takes it.
- **Never in a backup or a snapshot.** It is this device's record of what was done here, and **the words of what was deleted are in it**, which is the privacy cost: a phrase deleted because it was private stays on the device among the last fifty changes until newer ones push it out. The guide's *Saying something again* and the privacy policy's list of what is stored say so.
- **Not what Clear's Undo is.** The slot at the left end of the message line still offers Undo after Clear, for the words in the box; that is the message, and this is the board.
